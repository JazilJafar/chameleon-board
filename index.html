About

Chameleon Board is a flexible and adaptive Kanban board designed to help you organise your work and ideas effectively.

It has the following themes:

04

How to use

Get started with your board with just a few simple steps

01

Choose a theme

Pick a theme to make the workspace feel like yours.

02

Add your tasks

Create cards for your study goals, projects, or ideas so everything stays in one place.

03

Move things along

Drag cards between columns as you start working on them and finish them.

04

Keep your board fresh

Update your tasks as plans change and use your board to see what needs attention next.
